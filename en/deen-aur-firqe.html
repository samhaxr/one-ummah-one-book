In the sight of Allah the religion is only one, and it is Islam. The Quran calls the Muslims one ummah, commands them to hold together to the rope of Allah, and forbids them to be like those who broke their religion into pieces after clear proofs had come to them. In this lesson we look, in the light of the Quran, Sahih al-Bukhari and Sahih Muslim, at what the unity of the religion means, how sectarian splitting is described, and what guidance the Prophet ﷺ gave for times of division and trial.

1. The religion with Allah is one: Islam

Surah Al 'Imran declares plainly: «إِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْإِسْلَٰمُ», the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam. And it says: «وَمَن يَبْتَغِ غَيْرَ ٱلْإِسْلَٰمِ دِينًۭا فَلَن يُقْبَلَ مِنْهُ», whoever desires a religion other than Islam, it will never be accepted from him.

This religion has been completed: «ٱلْيَوْمَ أَكْمَلْتُ لَكُمْ دِينَكُمْ وَأَتْمَمْتُ عَلَيْكُمْ نِعْمَتِى وَرَضِيتُ لَكُمُ ٱلْإِسْلَٰمَ دِينًۭا», this day I have perfected your religion for you, completed My favour upon you and approved Islam as your religion. And Allah Himself named this ummah: «هُوَ سَمَّىٰكُمُ ٱلْمُسْلِمِينَ مِن قَبْلُ وَفِى هَٰذَا», He named you Muslims before and in this (Quran). So, according to the Quran, the name of identity is «Muslim».

The QuranSurah Aal-i-Imraan, 3:19

إِنَّ ٱلدِّينَ عِندَ ٱللَّهِ ٱلْإِسْلَٰمُ ۗ وَمَا ٱخْتَلَفَ ٱلَّذِينَ أُوتُوا۟ ٱلْكِتَٰبَ إِلَّا مِنۢ بَعْدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلْعِلْمُ بَغْيًۢا بَيْنَهُمْ ۗ وَمَن يَكْفُرْ بِـَٔايَٰتِ ٱللَّهِ فَإِنَّ ٱللَّهَ سَرِيعُ ٱلْحِسَابِ ﴿١٩﴾

Indeed, the religion in the sight of Allah is Islam. And those who were given the Scripture did not differ except after knowledge had come to them - out of jealous animosity between themselves. And whoever disbelieves in the verses of Allah, then indeed, Allah is swift in [taking] account. (19)

2. Holding together firmly to the rope of Allah

The basic command for the unity of the ummah is: «وَٱعْتَصِمُوا۟ بِحَبْلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًۭا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُوا۟», hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided. The same verse reminds that this unity is a favour of Allah: «إِذْ كُنتُمْ أَعْدَآءًۭ فَأَلَّفَ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِكُمْ فَأَصْبَحْتُم بِنِعْمَتِهِۦٓ إِخْوَٰنًۭا», you were enemies, then He brought your hearts together and by His favour you became brothers.

In Sahih Muslim the Prophet ﷺ said that Allah is pleased with three things for you: «فَيَرْضَى لَكُمْ أَنْ تَعْبُدُوهُ وَلاَ تُشْرِكُوا بِهِ شَيْئًا وَأَنْ تَعْتَصِمُوا بِحَبْلِ اللَّهِ جَمِيعًا وَلاَ تَفَرَّقُوا», that you worship Him and associate nothing with Him, and that you hold together to the rope of Allah and do not split. So tawhid and unity come side by side in one hadith. The Quran makes this brotherhood a bond of faith: «إِنَّمَا ٱلْمُؤْمِنُونَ إِخْوَةٌۭ فَأَصْلِحُوا۟ بَيْنَ أَخَوَيْكُمْ».

The QuranSurah Aal-i-Imraan, 3:103

وَٱعْتَصِمُوا۟ بِحَبْلِ ٱللَّهِ جَمِيعًۭا وَلَا تَفَرَّقُوا۟ ۚ وَٱذْكُرُوا۟ نِعْمَتَ ٱللَّهِ عَلَيْكُمْ إِذْ كُنتُمْ أَعْدَآءًۭ فَأَلَّفَ بَيْنَ قُلُوبِكُمْ فَأَصْبَحْتُم بِنِعْمَتِهِۦٓ إِخْوَٰنًۭا وَكُنتُمْ عَلَىٰ شَفَا حُفْرَةٍۢ مِّنَ ٱلنَّارِ فَأَنقَذَكُم مِّنْهَا ۗ كَذَٰلِكَ يُبَيِّنُ ٱللَّهُ لَكُمْ ءَايَٰتِهِۦ لَعَلَّكُمْ تَهْتَدُونَ ﴿١٠٣﴾

And hold firmly to the rope of Allah all together and do not become divided. And remember the favor of Allah upon you - when you were enemies and He brought your hearts together and you became, by His favor, brothers. And you were on the edge of a pit of the Fire, and He saved you from it. Thus does Allah make clear to you His verses that you may be guided. (103)

Translation: Saheeh International

3. One religion of all the prophets: 'establish the religion and do not split in it'

Surat ash-Shura tells us that Nuh, Ibrahim, Musa, 'Isa and Muhammad ﷺ were all given one and the same instruction: «أَنْ أَقِيمُوا۟ ٱلدِّينَ وَلَا تَتَفَرَّقُوا۟ فِيهِ», establish the religion and do not be divided in it. So avoiding division is not the command of one era only; it is the shared teaching of all the prophets.

In Surat al-Anbiya' Allah says: «إِنَّ هَٰذِهِۦٓ أُمَّتُكُمْ أُمَّةًۭ وَٰحِدَةًۭ وَأَنَا۠ رَبُّكُمْ فَٱعْبُدُونِ», this ummah of yours is one ummah and I am your Lord, so worship Me; then He describes what people did: «وَتَقَطَّعُوٓا۟ أَمْرَهُم بَيْنَهُمْ», they cut up their affair (religion) among themselves. Surat al-Mu'minun says the same with an addition: «فَتَقَطَّعُوٓا۟ أَمْرَهُم بَيْنَهُمْ زُبُرًۭا ۖ كُلُّ حِزْبٍۭ بِمَا لَدَيْهِمْ فَرِحُونَ», they divided their religion among them into sects, each faction rejoicing in what it has.

The QuranSurah Ash-Shura, 42:13

4. Those who split their religion and became factions

One of the sternest warnings in the Quran is: «إِنَّ ٱلَّذِينَ فَرَّقُوا۟ دِينَهُمْ وَكَانُوا۟ شِيَعًۭا لَّسْتَ مِنْهُمْ فِى شَىْءٍ», as for those who divided their religion and became sects, you (O Prophet) have nothing to do with them; their affair is with Allah.

Surat ar-Rum mentions this attitude together with the polytheists: «وَلَا تَكُونُوا۟ مِنَ ٱلْمُشْرِكِينَ» «مِنَ ٱلَّذِينَ فَرَّقُوا۟ دِينَهُمْ وَكَانُوا۟ شِيَعًۭا ۖ كُلُّ حِزْبٍۭ بِمَا لَدَيْهِمْ فَرِحُونَ», do not be among the polytheists, among those who divided their religion and became sects, every faction rejoicing in what it has.

And Allah says: «وَلَا تَكُونُوا۟ كَٱلَّذِينَ تَفَرَّقُوا۟ وَٱخْتَلَفُوا۟ مِنۢ بَعْدِ مَا جَآءَهُمُ ٱلْبَيِّنَٰتُ», do not be like those who became divided and differed after the clear proofs had come to them; for them is a great punishment. The worldly result of division is also stated: «وَلَا تَنَٰزَعُوا۟ فَتَفْشَلُوا۟ وَتَذْهَبَ رِيحُكُمْ», do not dispute with one another, or you will lose courage and your strength will depart.

5. One straight path, many side paths

Allah describes His path as one and the other paths as many: «وَأَنَّ هَٰذَا صِرَٰطِى مُسْتَقِيمًۭا فَٱتَّبِعُوهُ ۖ وَلَا تَتَّبِعُوا۟ ٱلسُّبُلَ فَتَفَرَّقَ بِكُمْ عَن سَبِيلِهِۦ», this is My path, which is straight, so follow it, and do not follow other ways, for they will separate you from His way.

The Prophet ﷺ used to say in his sermon: «فَإِنَّ خَيْرَ الْحَدِيثِ كِتَابُ اللَّهِ وَخَيْرُ الْهُدَى هُدَى مُحَمَّدٍ وَشَرُّ الأُمُورِ مُحْدَثَاتُهَا وَكُلُّ بِدْعَةٍ ضَلاَلَةٌ», the best speech is the Book of Allah, the best guidance is the guidance of Muhammad, the worst of affairs are newly invented matters, and every innovation is misguidance. And he said: «مَنْ أَحْدَثَ فِي أَمْرِنَا هَذَا مَا لَيْسَ فِيهِ فَهُوَ رَدٌّ», whoever introduces into this affair of ours something that is not part of it, it is rejected (the same hadith is in Sahih Muslim 1718).

When three men thought the Prophet's ﷺ worship too little and set themselves harsh practices of their own, he said: «فَمَنْ رَغِبَ عَنْ سُنَّتِي فَلَيْسَ مِنِّي», whoever turns away from my Sunnah is not of me. So the straight path is what is in the Book of Allah and the Sunnah of the Messenger ﷺ, and every way invented apart from it becomes a cause of separation.

6. The Prophet's guidance for times of trial

Hudhayfah ibn al-Yaman used to ask the Prophet ﷺ about evil. He ﷺ foretold people who would guide others without his way, and then said: «دُعَاةٌ عَلَى أَبْوَابِ جَهَنَّمَ، مَنْ أَجَابَهُمْ إِلَيْهَا قَذَفُوهُ فِيهَا», callers at the gates of Hell; whoever answers them, they will throw him into it. And he described them: «هُمْ مِنْ جِلْدَتِنَا، وَيَتَكَلَّمُونَ بِأَلْسِنَتِنَا», they will be from our own people and speak our own language.

Hudhayfah asked what he should do at such a time. He ﷺ said: «تَلْزَمُ جَمَاعَةَ الْمُسْلِمِينَ وَإِمَامَهُمْ», stick to the body of the Muslims and their imam. He asked: what if there is neither a body nor an imam? He said: «فَاعْتَزِلْ تِلْكَ الْفِرَقَ كُلَّهَا، وَلَوْ أَنْ تَعَضَّ بِأَصْلِ شَجَرَةٍ، حَتَّى يُدْرِكَكَ الْمَوْتُ، وَأَنْتَ عَلَى ذَلِكَ», then keep away from all those sects, even if you have to bite on the root of a tree, until death comes to you while you are in that state. In the narration of Sahih Muslim 1847 those people are described as: «قَوْمٌ يَسْتَنُّونَ بِغَيْرِ سُنَّتِي وَيَهْدُونَ بِغَيْرِ هَدْيِي».

Sahih Muslim Sahih Muslim, Hadith 1847 (sunnah.com: 1847a)

«قَوْمٌ يَسْتَنُّونَ بِغَيْرِ سُنَّتِي وَيَهْدُونَ بِغَيْرِ هَدْيِي … فَاعْتَزِلْ تِلْكَ الْفِرَقَ كُلَّهَا»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

It has been narrated on the authority of Hudhaifa b. al-Yaman who said: People used to ask the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) about the good times, but I used to ask him about bad times fearing lest they overtake me. I said: Messenger of Allah, we were in the midst of ignorance and evil, and then God brought us this good (time through Islam). Is there any bad time after this good one? He said: Yes. I asked: Will there be a good time again after that bad time? He said: Yes, but therein will be a hidden evil. I asked: What will be the evil hidden therein? He said: (That time will witness the rise of) the people who will adopt ways other than mine and seek guidance other than mine. You will know good points as well as bad points. I asked: Will there be a bad time after this good one? He said: Yes. (A time will come) when there will be people standing and inviting at the gates of Hell. Whoso responds to their call they will throw them into the fire. I said: Messenger of Allah, describe them for us. He said: All right. They will be a people having the same complexion as ours and speaking our language. I said: Messenger of Allah, what do you suggest if I happen to live in that time? He said: You should stick to the main body of the Muslims and their leader. I said: If they have no (such thing as the) main body and have no leader? He said: Separate yourself from all these factions, though you may have to eat the roots of trees (in a jungle) until death comes to you and you are in this state.

Sahih Muslim Sahih Muslim, Hadith 1848 (sunnah.com: 1848a)

«مَنْ خَرَجَ مِنَ الطَّاعَةِ وَفَارَقَ الْجَمَاعَةَ فَمَاتَ مَاتَ مِيتَةً جَاهِلِيَّةً»

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

It has been narrated on the authority of Abu Huraira that the Messenger of Allah (ﷺ) said: One who defected from obedience (to the Amir) and separated from the main body of the Muslims - if he died in that state-would die the death of one belonging to the days of Jahiliyya (i.e. would not die as a Muslim). One who fights under the banner of a people who are blind (to the cause for which they are fighting, i.e. do not know whether their cause is just or otherwise), who gets flared up with family pride, calls (people) to fight for their family honour, and supports his kith and kin (i.e. fights not for the cause of Allah but for the sake of this family or tribe) - if he is killed (in this fight), he dies as one belonging to the days of Jahiliyya. Whoso attacks my Ummah (indiscriminately) killing the righteous and the wicked of them, sparing not (even) those staunch in faith and fulfilling not his promise made with those who have been given a pledge of security - he has nothing to do with me and I have nothing to do with him.

7. Believers like one body and one building

In contrast to sectarian splitting, the picture of the ummah that the Prophet ﷺ gave is this: «تَرَى الْمُؤْمِنِينَ فِي تَرَاحُمِهِمْ وَتَوَادِّهِمْ وَتَعَاطُفِهِمْ كَمَثَلِ الْجَسَدِ إِذَا اشْتَكَى عُضْوًا تَدَاعَى لَهُ سَائِرُ جَسَدِهِ بِالسَّهَرِ وَالْحُمَّى», you see the believers in their mercy, love and kindness to one another like one body: when one limb is in pain, the whole body shares in sleeplessness and fever (Sahih Muslim 2586).

And he said: «إِنَّ الْمُؤْمِنَ لِلْمُؤْمِنِ كَالْبُنْيَانِ، يَشُدُّ بَعْضُهُ بَعْضًا», a believer to a believer is like a building whose parts strengthen one another, and he interlaced his fingers to show it. The Prophet ﷺ also gave this good news: «لاَ تَزَالُ طَائِفَةٌ مِنْ أُمَّتِي ظَاهِرِينَ عَلَى الْحَقِّ لاَ يَضُرُّهُمْ مَنْ خَذَلَهُمْ حَتَّى يَأْتِيَ أَمْرُ اللَّهِ», a group of my ummah will always remain upon the truth, prevailing; those who desert them will not harm them, until the command of Allah comes. The measure in this hadith is «the truth», not the name of any group.

English translation (full narration), source: sunnah.com

حَدَّثَنَا سَعِيدُ بْنُ مَنْصُورٍ ، وَأَبُو الرَّبِيعِ الْعَتَكِيُّ ، وَقُتَيْبَةُ بْنُ سَعِيدٍ ، قَالُوا حَدَّثَنَا حَمَّادٌ، - وَهُوَ ابْنُ زَيْدٍ - عَنْ أَيُّوبَ ، عَنْ أَبِي قِلاَبَةَ ، عَنْ أَبِي أَسْمَاءَ ، عَنْ ثَوْبَانَ ، قَالَ قَالَ رَسُولُ اللَّهِ صلى الله عليه وسلم ‏"‏ لاَ تَزَالُ طَائِفَةٌ مِنْ أُمَّتِي ظَاهِرِينَ عَلَى الْحَقِّ لاَ يَضُرُّهُمْ مَنْ خَذَلَهُمْ حَتَّى يَأْتِيَ أَمْرُ اللَّهِ وَهُمْ كَذَلِكَ ‏" ‏ ‏.‏ وَلَيْسَ فِي حَدِيثِ قُتَيْبَةَ ‏"‏ وَهُمْ كَذَلِكَ ‏"‏ ‏. ‏

Summary

  • The religion with Allah is Islam alone; it has been completed, and Allah named this ummah «Muslims» (Al 'Imran 3:19, 85; al-Ma'idah 5:3; al-Hajj 22:78).
  • Holding together to the rope of Allah and not splitting is something Allah is pleased with (Al 'Imran 3:103; Sahih Muslim 1715).
  • All the prophets were instructed to establish the religion and not split in it; the ummah is one (ash-Shura 42:13; al-Anbiya' 21:92; al-Mu'minun 23:52).
  • The Prophet ﷺ has nothing to do with those who split their religion into sects; each faction rejoices in what it has (al-An'am 6:159; ar-Rum 30:32).
  • The straight path is one; a newly invented matter in the religion is rejected (al-An'am 6:153; Sahih Muslim 867; Sahih al-Bukhari 2697).
  • In times of trial, stay with the body of the Muslims and their imam; otherwise keep away from all the sects (Sahih al-Bukhari 7084; Sahih Muslim 1847).
  • Believers are like one body and one building (Sahih al-Bukhari 6011, 481).
